# Faster crafting

A data pack that halves the ingredients and the time of three production
recipes: bronze bars at the smelter, fired bricks at the kiln and flat bread at
the oven.

The idea comes from the recipe part of
[Miscellaneous Tweaks](https://www.nexusmods.com/enshrouded/mods/67) on Nexus
Mods. This pack shares no code or data with it.
