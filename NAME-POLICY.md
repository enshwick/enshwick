# Name policy

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers Enshwick's code. It does not cover the name
Enshwick. This policy says who may ship a build under that name.

## Who may build and ship the code?

Anyone may build, change and ship the code under the MIT licence. Keep the
copyright and licence notice with every copy.

## What is an official build?

An official build is the files of an `enshwick.zip` from the
[releases page](https://github.com/enshwick/enshwick/releases), unchanged. A
build you compiled yourself, or changed in any way, is not an official build.

## Who may ship a build under the name Enshwick?

Anyone may bundle an official build under the name Enshwick. Copying its files
into your own archive is fine.

## What if your build is not an official build?

Then do one of these:

- Give it a name that does not call it Enshwick.
- Call it an unofficial build of Enshwick, and link the
  [official download](https://github.com/enshwick/enshwick/releases).

## Why does the name matter?

Players ask us for help with the build they run. We can vouch only for a build
we released. A clear name tells players where their build came from.

## Who answers questions about the name?

Write to [contact@enshwick.com](mailto:contact@enshwick.com).
