### Fixed

- In a world, a loader or mod window or dialog shows the mouse cursor and
  takes clicks, and the camera and the character no longer move under it.
  Escape closes a loader dialog, and steps back in a carried window: it ends
  the typing, then leaves an open form, then closes the window, without
  opening the pause menu.
- A page of the Mods screen, and the list after it, opens at its top
  instead of where the page before it was scrolled.
- Mod settings sliders show their value: a whole number for whole settings,
  else two decimals.
- A click on a text setting of the Mods screen starts the typing.
- A disabled recipe leaves the crafting menus (by hand, at stations and at
  survivors); before, only the server refused it.
