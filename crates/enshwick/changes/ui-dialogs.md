### Fixed

- Loader: a loader or mod dialog over a loader or mod window takes the
  mouse and the keys alone. A click on its buttons answers it, and nothing
  in the window under it reacts: no second dialog from a button behind it,
  no page change from the arrow keys or Enter.
- Loader: a mod's item slot shows the game's hover look under the cursor,
  as the inventory's own slots do, and joins its window's gamepad
  navigation.
- Loader: the interface demo's HUD samples stay on the HUD after its window
  closes, until they are turned off again or the world ends; a reopened
  demo shows the samples' toggle and bar as they were.
