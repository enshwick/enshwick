# enshwick-content

Internal part of [Enshwick](https://github.com/enshwick/enshwick); depend on
[`enshwick`](https://crates.io/crates/enshwick), which re-exports every item
here under its own paths.

The content domain: the game's items, recipes, workshops, templates and the
rest a mod adds or changes, the rates that scale them, and the generated
typed items they read.
