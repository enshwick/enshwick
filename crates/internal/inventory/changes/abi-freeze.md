### Changed

- **Breaking:** `stats::builtin::DAMAGE_SHROUD` and `RESISTANCE_SHROUD` are
  `DAMAGE_FOG` and `RESISTANCE_FOG`, after the game's `MagicalFog` damage,
  with the ids `enshwick:damage_fog` and `enshwick:resistance_fog`. Stat
  modifiers a world saved and item stats under the old ids no longer apply
  (#1282).
