# enshwick-inventory

Internal part of [Enshwick](https://github.com/enshwick/enshwick); depend on
[`enshwick`](https://crates.io/crates/enshwick), which re-exports every item
here under its own paths.

The inventory domain: inventories and the items in them, the data items and
entities carry, affixes and stats, damage and status effects, survivors and
loot.
